What we believe

Every conversation we have — with a candidate or a company — should be one they're glad they had.

Our mission

We help India's product companies, startups, GCCs and IT services organizations hire well by doing the unglamorous work most recruiters skip: actually listening. We take the time to understand what a team is really struggling with, and we treat every candidate — hired or not — like a person, not a CV.

When both sides feel heard, the right matches happen on their own.

How we work

The values
we hold to.

  1. 01

    Candidates first

    Every candidate leaves better than they came.

    Hired or not, every person who talks to us gets honesty, clarity, and respect for their time.

  2. 02

    Diagnose, then deliver

    Listen first, solve fast.

    We don't pitch before we understand. We dig into the real pain behind the job description, then move quickly to fix it.

  3. 03

    Quality over quantity

    Shortlist, don't shovel.

    We send people we'd vouch for, not profiles we found. Fewer, better, right.

  4. 04

    Radical honesty

    Tell the truth, even when it costs us.

    We'll tell a client when a role is mis-scoped or a candidate isn't right — even when silence would close the deal faster.

  5. 05

    Full accountability

    Own the outcome.

    We're not done when the offer is accepted. We're done when the person is thriving and the company is glad they trusted us.
